## Full description (≤4000)

```text
SpendTracker works out where your money went from the SMS alerts your bank already sends. No typing every expense, no account to create, and nothing leaves your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
• Turn on SMS reading and the app picks up bank and UPI transaction alerts as they arrive. The first time, it also imports the last 180 days from your inbox.
• From each alert it reads the amount, money in or out, the payee or UPI ID, the bank, the last digits of the account or card, the reference number and the available balance.
• OTPs, offers, payment requests, reminders, failed payments and messages from personal numbers are skipped and never stored.

SEE WHERE IT WENT
• Home shows how much is left of your monthly budget or your income, and where the money went by category.
• Make your own categories, like Pets or Gym, and rename or delete them any time.
• Fits how you earn: salary (payday to payday), business, freelance work, an allowance, or a mix.
• Any date range: this or last pay cycle, this or last month, the last 3 months, or any dates on a calendar.
• Activity lists every transaction by day, with search and filters.
• Reports show ring and bar charts for expenses and income, and how each category changed from the previous period.
• Download a report as a PDF or a CSV for Excel or Google Sheets.

BUDGETS, GOALS AND REMINDERS
• Set a monthly budget, plus budgets for single categories, and see how much is left of each.
• Set a savings goal and watch it fill up as you move money into savings and investments.
• Finds payments that repeat, like rent, EMIs and subscriptions, and can remind you the day before one is due.
• Tips on Home when a category goes over budget or jumps compared with the last period.
• Optional weekly summary of your spending.
• Home-screen widget with this month's spending, top category and income.
• Long-press the app icon to add an expense or income in one tap.

ADD THINGS YOURSELF
• Add cash spends or income by hand, with a category, payment method, note and a receipt photo.
• Prefer not to give SMS access? Track everything by hand instead.

COUNTED ONCE, CORRECTLY
• Credit card bill payments and transfers between your own accounts are shown but not counted as spending.
• Cash you spend is matched to your ATM withdrawal, so it isn't counted twice.
• Change a payment's category once and the app remembers it for that payee.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission, so the app cannot send your data anywhere.
• No sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no cloud backup.
• Stop SMS reading or delete all data at any time from Settings.

WHY SMS ACCESS
SpendTracker asks for SMS access only to find bank and UPI transaction alerts and turn them into your spending history, on your phone. This is the app's core feature. You can say no and still add everything by hand.

Built for Indian bank and UPI SMS formats.
```
